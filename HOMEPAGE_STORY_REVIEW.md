# Homepage storytelling review — 8 October 2026

Integrated on main v0.4.7 from 6c7a517, retaining its Roots discovery and production contact implementation.

## Editorial approach

Visitor invitation → reason for the project → founder's four-part journey → introductory practice → Farm & Botanicals / Eco-Retreat / Academy → team → existing library → roadmap → beginner questions → contact.

Biography and educational milestones are supplied by the user, not independently verified credentials. Institutional links provide context; they do not establish certification, endorsement or partnership. No invented dates, testimonials, student totals or therapeutic outcomes.

Inspiration: Bidicomed's soil-and-people storytelling rhythm; Học Thiền Đà Nẵng's origin-to-community progression; Diviners' connection between intention, programmes and learning resources. Wording is original.

## Review as a first-time learner

- Who welcomes me? Four real supplied photographs, the founder's name and grounded biography answer this.
- Can I relate without repeating the founder's life? The story moves to the visitor's choices and practical steps.
- What can I do now? Existing bilingual readings and a one-minute practice have working internal links.
- Are products, retreats and courses open? They are explicitly development directions, with no invented dates or prices.
- Must I adopt a belief? The FAQ invites questions and personal observation.
- Does healing promise a cure? The FAQ defines self-care and avoids guarantees.
- Is the page overly biographical? Four concise chapters, then a return to practice and community benefit.
- What still needs source confirmation? The exact official institution name and any documentary evidence; future schedules, location and fees.
